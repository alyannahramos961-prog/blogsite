LEARNING AS I GO

Learning one
step at a time.

By Alyannah Esteban · October 5, 2026

Starting something new can make me feel as if I should already know how to do it. A blank page, a new subject, or an unfamiliar skill can seem much bigger when I imagine the whole journey at once.

Recently, I decided to practice a little each day instead of waiting until I felt completely ready. The first attempts were messy. I made mistakes, looked things up, tried again, and slowly began to recognize patterns that had seemed confusing before.

Progress is easy to miss

Learning rarely feels like a straight line. Some days bring a satisfying breakthrough; others are mostly repetition. But small efforts add up. Looking back at my early notes helped me see just how much I had figured out without noticing it in the moment.

It also helped to ask questions and let myself be a beginner. I don't have to be embarrassed about not knowing something yet. Being curious is a good enough reason to start.

“You can begin before you feel ready.”

So here's a reminder for me—and maybe for you, too: choose one small next step. Do that, and then decide what comes after. That's how big things get made.
