OUTDOOR ADVENTURES

Chasing Views and
Fresh Mountain Air.

By Alyannah Esteban

Sometimes the best reset doesn't require a complicated itinerary—just a drive out toward the hills, fresh air, and wide-open views. Standing surrounded by trees and overlooking rolling green mountains under a bright, cloud-streaked sky makes all the daily stress feel a little smaller.

Spending time outdoors with good company reminds you how refreshing it is to disconnect for a few hours. Whether it's feeling the cool breeze, taking in the scenery, or capturing a quick photo before heading back down, moments like these are always worth keeping in the journal.
